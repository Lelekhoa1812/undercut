# Community conduct

Help contributors learn without being humiliated. Be specific, patient, considerate, and evidence-led. Challenge behavior and code, not personal competence. Disagreement is welcome; harassment, threats, intimidation, discriminatory abuse, and disclosure of private information are not.

Report issues privately to the repository maintainer when possible. Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content and restrict participation to keep discussion constructive. Do not submit security vulnerabilities or credentials to public issue threads; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
