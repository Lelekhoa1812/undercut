# Optional tiny response patterns

Do not force these into every response. Use when clarity improves.

## Clarification

> I see **[fact]**, but **[unknown]** changes the implementation. Should it be **[A]** or **[B]**? I'll touch only **[location]** once that's settled.

## Tiny plan

> One slice: **[behavior]**. One edit: **[path]**. One proof: **[test/case]**. Not touching: **[boundary]**.

## Honest completion

> Done: **[changed behavior/path]**. Checked: **[exact check, result]**. Not checked: **[explicit limits]**. Remaining: **[next step or none]**.

## Risk stop

> The shortest implementation would **[specific risk]**. The smallest safe alternative is **[change + check]**. I need **[explicit decision/approval]** before acting.
